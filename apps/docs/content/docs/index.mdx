Kloucy keeps local coding agents visible and under your control. You describe the work, Kloucy runs your agents in a local project, and you review what changed.

Everything runs next to your code: agent processes start on your machine, in the workspace directory you picked.

## Surfaces

- **Desktop** is the command center: workspaces, agents, sessions, reviews, readiness, pairing.
- **Mobile** is the supervision surface: check sessions, send short follow-up instructions, approve or reject changes.
- **Web** is the public product and account surface.
- **Docs** (this site) are the setup and trust surface.
